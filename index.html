About

My career started underwater. I spent nine years as a U.S. Navy diver out of Pearl Harbor — ship's husbandry, salvage and recovery from Southeast Asia to the Middle East, and emergency medical response for 7th fleet. Then nine more in electrical and electronic signals work, running squadron-wide casualty data collection and managing teams of technicians.

After the Navy I worked as a senior field applications specialist for Lutron, then ran my own business technology consultancy in Los Angeles. Along the way I picked up a data analytics fundamentals from a bootcamp at USC, kept coding through Pasadena City College, and shipped production tooling at KLA.

Today I'm an undergraduate Data Science student at the University of Arkansas — an interdisciplinary program across the Business, Science, and Engineering colleges. I program in Python, JavaScript, and C++, with a focus on agricultural commodity data, statistical analysis, and turning rich datasets into things you can actually click on. Lately that's been coffee. A lot of coffee.

Hobbies, by way of explanation: ♆ diving, 🪂 skydiving, 🏄 surfing, 🏂 snowboarding, 🧘 yoga, 💃🏽🕺 salsa & swing, and — for the long game — fermenting kombucha in a glass dispenser on my counter.
